About Me

Marketer by Training, Builder by Habit

I am a Communication Studies graduate who learned marketing by doing it with real money on the line: selling property online, growing social accounts, running a marketplace store, and building my own motorcycle-wash brand from an empty plot of land to two outlets.

Who Am I?

My path started in 2020 selling property through Facebook, Google Ads, and OLX, where I closed nearly 100 sales and learned that good marketing always ends in a real conversation. Since then I have grown social accounts for training brands, written 400+ SEO articles, run the Aquline store on Shopee and TikTok Shop, facilitated classes for 50+ people at a time, and coordinated a live music event.

Today I run 123 Motowash, a brand I created myself, from the name and logo to the SOPs, pricing, promos, and a team of 10+ people. Daily customer volume is now about 3× our first month, the first outlet paid for itself in about 7 months, and a second outlet opened in May 2026.

I believe creativity should be backed by data, and data should be easy to act on. That is why I build my own tools, like an Android recap app and a booking website, so the team spends less time on admin and more time on customers.
